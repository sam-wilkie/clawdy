## Vinnie

Vinnie (formerly Mochi) is an original character designed by Sam Wilkie. It is
not a third-party mascot and not fan art. The copyright covers the character
itself, not only the files that draw it: its name, its design (the white round
body with a red crescent, three close-set eyes and mittens), its poses and
animations, and its personality as described in [docs/vinnie.md](docs/vinnie.md).

It includes, in any version, redraw or format:

- the pet art in `desktop/renderer/assets/monster/` and
  `hotbar/ui/assets/pets/monster-idle.svg`;
- the presentation sheet `assets/readme/vinnie-sheet.png`;
- the character sheet `docs/vinnie.md`;
- any image, sprite, sticker or animation of Vinnie made from them.

The code that generates or checks this art (`tools/monster_pixel.py`,
`tools/vinnie_sheet.py`, `tools/pet_check.py`) is source code under the
[LICENSE](LICENSE). That license covers the code only: it grants no right to
Vinnie, and art produced with the tools to depict Vinnie stays under this file.
