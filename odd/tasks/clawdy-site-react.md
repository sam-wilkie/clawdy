# Clawdy site (React)

Rebuild the Clawdy landing as a React app that follows the flow of the reference site, with Vinnie as the pet.
Branch: `feat/site-react` (from `feat/vinnie-pet`). Delivery: ask-on-risk. Runner: `pnpm test` (vitest) in `site/`. Engram mirror: `odd/clawdy-site-react/tasks`.

## Specs

- S1 "como lo mejoramos con react? quiero que sea mas movil": a React app (Vite, pnpm) in a new versioned folder `site/`; mobile first, works at 390 px wide with touch. User confirmed the plan with "hazlo". The old `website/` prototype stays untouched and ignored.
- S2 "estabamos basados en esta pagina ... aun faltan muchas cosas como mi github en la esquina, muchas animaciones, la interfaz de mas correctamente, las posiciones etc...": the flow and positions follow https://clawdondesk.com, observed with REA ("USA https://github.com/morluto/rea para que tengamos el flujo similar") and Playwright on 2026-10-24 at 1280x720.
- S3 Flow observed: intro on a dark screen (Vinnie above, cursor types the wordmark, flash, delete, centre) -> iris opens the desk -> stage (Vinnie, hint pill under it, drop zone bottom-left, dot grid, flower patch) -> drag Vinnie into the drop zone -> terminal window appears -> typed prompt, "thinking", Bash tool, "Do you want to proceed?" with 3 options -> permission card -> Allow opens the second page; Deny makes Vinnie plead once (card text changes, hearts, option 3 selected), a second Deny ends in the exit screen.
- S4 Geometry at 1280x720 (REA screenshot): terminal 562x317 at (359,267), dark, three macOS dots, title "clawd@desk: ~"; behind the permission card the terminal is dimmed and blurred; permission card 359x192 at (806,245): title "Permission Request", pill "BASH", code box, buttons Allow / Deny; Vinnie waits at about (938,541) to the right of the terminal; flower patch bottom-left of the terminal; drop zone 128x149 at (230,488); hint pill about 168x36 under Vinnie; author link bottom-right at (1080,687).
- S5 Second page (Allow): "Clawd on Desk" / "A little company for your coding day." become the Clawdy title and a Spanish subtitle; grid of 6 cards with Vinnie animations and captions Reading, Thinking, Typing, Wizard, 1 Subagent, 2+ Subagents (3 columns on desktop, fewer on phones); "Play from start" button top-right; a GitHub pill at the bottom linking to the repo.
- S6 "mi github en la esquina": a link `github.com/sam-wilkie` fixed bottom-right on every screen, pointing to https://github.com/sam-wilkie/clawdy. Never the original author's link.
- S7 "los Colores deberian ser, el rojo, negro para contraste y blanco no puro" + "el rojo de vinnie en este caso me gusta como mas": Vinnie red `#DE4553`, black `#000000` and ink `#0A0A0A`, snow `#FAFAFA`. Red is for fills and accents; small text stays ink.
- S8 "muchas animaciones": walk-in, happy on click, dizzy after two circles of the pointer, sleep after idle and wake on movement, dashed arc with a hand from Vinnie to the drop zone, flower patch, absorb into the terminal, typed terminal lines and thinking spinner, permission card pop-in, pleading on first Deny, shades on exit, staggered showcase cards. `prefers-reduced-motion` skips the intro and long motion.
- S9 Copy is Spanish (the previous prototype's UI language). Code and comments are English.
- S10 Not copied from the reference: its code and art are AGPL-3.0. Behaviour, geometry and flow are rebuilt from observation; art is Vinnie's SVGs from `desktop/renderer/assets/monster/`.
- S12 "el favicon no funciona bien": the tab icon is Vinnie's face as PNG/ICO (32, 16, and a 180 px apple-touch icon), not the animated 500 px SVG.
- S13 "en la pagina que te di el muñeco reacciona al click, cierra los ojitos cute, y se puede dejar en cualquier parte": a click closes Vinnie's eyes (happy) and makes the flowers bloom around it; after a drag Vinnie stays wherever it is released (the flowers follow, the drop zone stays visible, the hint is gone), unless it lands on the drop zone. Observed on the reference with Playwright.
- S14 "esta muy grande el tamaño de vinnie en la pantalla": Vinnie's box is clamp(150px, 26vmin, 230px) (187 px at 1280x720, was 252).
- S15 "que pasa con la pantalla de inicio porq ya no carga, se supone que al recargar tierne que volver a verse": the intro plays on every load (the reference stores nothing and replays it on reload); `?intro=0` skips it, reduced motion skips it. No localStorage flag.
- S16 "si se sacude a vinnie deberia marearse asi como pasa en el desktop": shaking Vinnie while dragging makes it dizzy, with the desktop pet's rule (moves of 6 px or more, 3 reversals within 1 s, dizzy for 2 s; `desktop/renderer/renderer.js` detectShake).
- S17 "porq se alegra al darle que no, faltan las animaciones de error": on Deny, Vinnie shows its `error` animation (X eyes, ERROR tag), not `happy`; no hearts. The plea text stays.
- S18 "cuando se le da al boton de empzar de nuevo tiene que empezar a la pantalla de inicio": "Volver a empezar" (showcase and exit) goes back to the intro; with `?intro=0` or reduced motion it goes to the stage.
- S19 "la zona de sueltame aqui que aparezca todo el rato": the drop zone is visible the whole time on the stage (hidden during the intro and once Vinnie is docked).
- S20 "el efecto del mouse debe aparecer siempre ... me gusatria que entraras a 21st ... algo tipo pixel o similar con colores tipo rojiso ... lo que hiciste en los heros del blog pero en vez de negro a rojo, que sea blanco a rojo pero no se vea como una mancha": an always-on pointer effect (not tied to clicking Vinnie), pixel cells from near white to Vinnie red, found on 21st: Grid Pulse (id 27200, carolinaraulino), the component the Wilkiedevs blog GridPulse is based on. Cells of 18 px on the 36 px dot grid, five discrete shades, a 1 px gap, so it reads as pixels and not as a blot.
- S21 "la mascota tambien debe seguir flkuidamente el mouse con la mirada": Vinnie's face follows the pointer with easing (up to 3 art pixels sideways, 1.5 up or down) while idle.
- S22 "los pixeles son muy grandes y la estelea muy larga tambien": the pointer effect uses 9 px cells (a quarter of the 36 px dot grid), a reach of 5 cells and fades out in about 0.3 s; nothing is lit 0.7 s after the pointer stops.
- S23 "deberias eliminar la anterior que existia, la que queda debajo del pet": the flower patch under Vinnie is removed (component, module, tests, CSS).
- S24 "claude se queda en error no despierta para esperar que seleccione otra opcion": after the first Deny Vinnie shows `error` (2.2 s), then `wake` (1.6 s), then `waiting_reply` until the next answer. When the card first appears: `idea` (2.4 s) then `waiting_reply`. Observed on the reference: it lies down with ERROR, then wakes.
- S25 "y no solo sobre el fondo claro": the pointer effect also draws over the dark intro, exit and terminal (canvas above them, below Vinnie, the card and the showcase).
- S26 "varias animaciones que faltam tambien de cuando se esconde detras de la pantalla del terminal del mac, podrias crea la animacion con claude opus 5.5": Vinnie slips under the terminal window's left edge while the agent thinks and comes out at its right edge when the card is about to appear; the two sprites (`slip-in.svg`, `slip-out.svg`) are drawn by an Opus agent (model `opus`).
- S27 "la animacion se siente sosa, porq en la que te pase se asome y va mirando": the hide/emerge sequence needs a PEEK beat like the reference: after slipping under the window, Vinnie peeks out at its edge and looks around (eyes left, right, up, a blink) before coming out. Today it only slides under, crosses hidden and slips out. Candidate art: `mini_clawd` (Vinnie's peeking face, already in `desktop/renderer/assets/monster/`) or a new sprite made by the Opus agent. PENDING.
- S28 "no debe aparecer el efecto del mouse sobre el fondo negro, se ver feo, y el efecto debe quedar bien en backgroung no sobre la terminal u otro elemento similar": supersedes S25. The pointer effect lives only on the snow background: canvas back at `z-index: 0` (below the terminal, intro, exit, card and showcase), nothing over black. PENDING (one CSS line in `site/src/App.css`, `.pulse`).
- S11 Assumption (not asked): no extra menu or header; the "navigation" is the flow itself plus "Play from start" and the GitHub links.
